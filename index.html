Why I'm building this

For over a decade, I've worked inside households for ultra-high-net-worth families here in New York City. I've seen up close everything it takes to run a home — schedules, staff, guests, repairs, constant coordination.

Then I went back home and talked to a few friends — a working mom juggling her job and her household, sometimes with help, sometimes without, and a busy professional without kids who was just as overwhelmed keeping her own life together. Listening to them, I realized something: this isn't just a problem for families with full staff, or for moms, or for any one kind of household. It's a problem for every busy woman holding things together in her head.

Two completely different worlds. The same invisible weight. That's when it hit me — everyone could use a House Manager. Not because they can't handle it, but because no one should have to carry it alone.

That's why I'm building BRIA.

— Founder, BRIA
